1. The service

Sello is a national park stamp journal. With the App you can:

  • pick national parks, write your reasons and up to three personal objectives, and plan a departure date;
  • check in, write a debrief or a Quick Log, attach photos, and mint a Gold, Silver, Bronze or Plain park stamp;
  • track yearly seasons and your lifetime park ledger;
  • add keepsakes to a stamp: a 10-second Sound Rubbing, a wax-sealed Return Letter that opens when you return to the same park, and quarterly Field Order insignia;
  • generate printable passport folio plates and season recaps as PNG images.

"Passport" in Sello means your park stamp collection only. Sello stamps, folios and keepsakes are personal mementos. They are not travel documents and are not issued, endorsed or recognized by the National Park Service or any government agency.
