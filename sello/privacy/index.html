Sello Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sello ("Sello", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Sello iOS app (Bundle ID com.sello.uourhf, the "App"). Sello is a national park stamp journal: you plan park quests, log visits, earn collectible park stamps, add keepsakes such as return letters and sound rubbings, and generate printable passport folio plates.

"Passport" in Sello refers only to your national park stamp collection. Sello never asks for a government passport, ID document, legal name, date of birth or nationality.

1. Information you provide

Category Examples Why it is used
Profile An anonymous explorer name and password generated by Quick Login To keep your quests, stamps and Park Credits together under one profile
Journal content Park quests, objectives, visit dates, routes, wildlife notes, companions, miles, notes and drafts To build your quest board, stamps, seasons and folio plates

Journal content, photos, keepsakes, drafts, generated folio plates and your Park Credit ledger are kept in the App's private storage on your device. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

2. Permissions

Sello asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change any permission later in iOS Settings.

  • Camera — requested when you tap Take Photo in a debrief or Quick Log entry, to capture a park photo for that visit.
  • Photo Library — Choose Photo uses the system photo picker, which only gives Sello the single photo you select. Saving a folio plate, stamp or letter image uses add-only access, requested when you tap Save.
  • Microphone — requested when you tap Speak Notes to dictate, or Record to capture a 10-second Sound Rubbing. The microphone is active only while you dictate or record.
  • Speech Recognition — requested the first time you use Speak Notes. Sello asks iOS to recognize speech on the device when your iPhone supports it. If on-device recognition is unavailable, iOS may process the audio through Apple's speech recognition service under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Notifications — Sello asks for notification permission the first time you reach the Quest Board. You may decline; every feature keeps working.

Sello does not request location access and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

3. Photos and audio

  • Photos you add are copied into the App's private storage so the original in your library is never modified.
  • Sound Rubbings are 10-second ambient clips. Their loudness is drawn as a ring around the stamp. You can preview, re-record or remove a clip at any time from the stamp's Keepsakes.
  • Removing a photo or Sound Rubbing from a visit deletes that file from the App's storage.

4. Facial data

4.1 What is collected

Sello does not collect, create or store facial geometry, faceprints, face templates or any other biometric identifiers. Sello does not perform face detection, face recognition or face analysis. The only content that may show a face is a photo you choose to attach to a visit entry, for example a picture of yourself at a trailhead.

4.2 How it is used

A photo you attach is used only to display your visit entry, your stamp details and the folio plates you choose to generate. Sello does not analyze the people shown in photos.

4.3 Sharing and disclosure

Photos are not shared with us or with any third party. A photo leaves the App only when you choose to export it yourself, for example by saving a folio plate to Photos or by using the iOS share sheet.

4.4 Where it is stored and how long it is kept

Attached photos are stored in the App's private storage on your device for as long as the visit entry exists. Removing the photo from the entry, or using Delete Account, deletes it.

4.5 Scope of this section

This Section 4 is Sello's complete explanation of the collection, use, disclosure, sharing and retention of any images that may include a face.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store processes all purchases. Sello never receives or stores your card or payment details.
  • Apple speech recognition may be used by iOS for dictation as described in Section 2.
  • Sello does not use any third-party artificial intelligence service. No prompts, photos, audio or journal content are sent to an AI provider.
  • Sello does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. In-app purchases

Park Credits are consumable in-app purchases sold through the App Store. One Park Credit is used each time you generate a passport folio or season recap; previews are always free. Your credit balance and a ledger of purchases and uses are kept with your profile. Purchases are subject to Apple's terms, and refunds are handled by Apple.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Log Out (Settings) ends your session. Your journal remains available the next time you sign in with Quick Login on this device.
  • Delete Account (Settings, with a two-step confirmation) permanently erases your explorer profile, quests, stamps, seasons, drafts, photos, Sound Rubbings, return letters, field order claims, generated folio plates, Park Credit balance and purchase ledger. The App then returns to its first-launch state. Unused Park Credits cannot be restored after deletion.
  • Images you previously saved to your Photos library stay in Photos until you delete them there.

8. Children's privacy

Sello is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

We use the protections provided by iOS for app storage. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device protected with a passcode.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
